# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
1. Staff
2. Head of department
3. General Manager
4. Director

## What exactly is going wrong?
1. Multiple department key in different details for each asset from their respective laptop.
2. The asset details is seperated by company name and must be in Finance department and save in local disk making it unable to be update from another company or department.
3. If there's an update from IT department, a verbal conversation and physical movement is needed to update only a few details.

## Why does it matter?
1. Data redundancy can be occured due to each asset is stored in different disk.
2. Cannot view the correct updated asset details when neceessary.
3. Time consuming if movement is needed just to update a few details.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
1. Users need to go Finance that take up so much time just to update a few details and to avoid data redundancy.
2. Head of department and general manager cannot correctly audit or observe the latest update for certain asset due to multiple file storing in different laptop.
3. All the department need a centralize system that connect all the assets details that can be updated by certain department.
